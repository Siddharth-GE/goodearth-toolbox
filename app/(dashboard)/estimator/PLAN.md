# Estimator — the rules

Grant `/estimator`. Migrations `0074`–`0088`, `0096`–`0098` and `0118` (`0078`–`0081` shared with Indents and Inventory; `0113`'s `decide_indent_line` is the Estimator's answer on Indents' table).

What a villa costs to build. Works come from the Masters vocabulary (`0073`); this tool adds what each is measured in, what its labour costs and what it consumes. Selections and Budgets are interiors and stay out of it.

## The shape (founder, 2026-09-26)

**Every villa is different** — "finishes are different, foundations can be different" — so there is no house type villas follow. What is shared is **how a work is priced**, never how much of it a villa has.

- **Rate book** (the Works tab): per work, its unit, labour ₹/unit and materials per unit at Masters' prices. Mixes are their own tab. Units and labour rates come from the founder's masters workbook (`scripts/import-masters-workbook.ts`); Superstructure and MEP are priced as **packages**: each stage's share of the package rate is its own work, `SS.01.00` "… — package per sqft" (12% × 230 = ₹27.6), measured in sqft of built-up area; the sub-works beneath carry no rate (`masters/PLAN.md`).
- **Villa**: one working estimate, its own list of works, each measured for that villa. It follows the rate book unless it has its own labour rate, materials or price for a work — grey follows the rate book, black is this villa's own, ↺ puts it back. **A work may sit on it more than once** (`0157`, team's additions 2026-10-26): Shutter for W1 and for W2, each row named, measured and priced on its own (its own mix, materials and labour). **Duplicate** copies a row, and adding works skips a work already on the estimate. A work the rate book has not set up is set up from its row (**Set up here**: the unit, and the labour rate if known, saved for the work).
- **Official**: a numbered, frozen copy (EST/…/NNN) — what the stores and site check against.
- **Site check**: the indents waiting, then a card per villa; a villa opens its own check.

## The founder's decisions

1. **Materials ARE the items master** (`0086`: "all materials are exactly the same as in the items master"). The rate is `items.indicative_price`, edited in Masters; recipe quantities are in the item's own unit, so nothing converts and issued-vs-estimated compares exactly. `estimator_materials` is retired in place and never read: a recipe row from before `0086` counts for nothing and says so; a frozen takeoff row from before it shows by its frozen name. `estimate_takeoff_facts` still bridges both generations for the site tools behind one unchanged column list.
2. **A rate explains itself.** On a draft every rate is a button opening its build-up for this villa — labour, what one unit uses, what each material costs — each figure marked _Rate book_ or _This villa_. A different foundation is a different work (the list has rubble / isolated / pile); a different finish is **Swap** on the villa's row, the rate book's material standing in as an allowance until the choice is made. Editing a villa's materials is **copy-on-write** (`changeLineRecipe`): the first change copies the rate book's list onto the line. Labour varies on the line, a material price on the estimate (one row per material, so two works cannot disagree about cement), each an override where blank means "follow the master" and 0 is a real rate (`0087`/`0088`). Someone holding `/masters` can set a missing price from the same panel (`setItemPrice`). The rate book lists works **used but not priced** and offers **Copy this rate to…** with the same work on other floors ticked (`floorTwins`).
3. **Mixes are named and reusable** — change M20 once and every concrete work follows. A recipe may hold both mixes and direct materials. A mix may carry a free-text group (`0154`, matched whatever its case); the Mixes list groups, searches (materials inside included) and filters every mix in memory, since there are few.
4. **One working estimate per villa; Make official takes a frozen copy** (`0098`). `make_estimate_official` copies the working estimate with everything under it, writes the snapshot the app computed, refuses if it no longer matches, and mints EST/…/NNN superseding the previous official — one transaction; the working estimate stays open. **An official is withdrawn** (`0144`: status `cancelled`, with a reason, stamped) while no indent that isn't cancelled draws on it; the villa then has no official until the working estimate is made official again, and every view, reading `status = 'submitted'`, leaves it out by itself. Supervisors then plans the villa from the full stage list, so the dialog says so. An indent line written in the same instant as a withdraw can still anchor on it, as with a supersede: `indent_lines_estimate_anchor` reads the view without a lock, and an /indents holder cannot take one on the estimate. A villa starts blank or from another estimate (`start_villa_estimate`: its works to measure, or ticked, everything). Revise, Copy to villa and templates are gone from the screens.
5. **Measure first.** A work can be listed before it is measured (`0097`: `qty` null = "to measure"); Make official refuses while any is, naming them. Works can be added in bulk, set up or not, so the rate book never has to come first.
6. **A line's quantity can come from a measurement sheet** (`0096`): rows of Nos × Length × Breadth × Depth, a blank box not used. **A door, window or other opening is a Deduct row** (`0132`, founder 2026-10-22): measured with positive boxes like any row and taken off the total, so a typed minus is still refused and the sheet reads plainly; a model row is never one, and a sheet whose deductions reach zero or less is refused. After every row change the action re-sums the sheet through `calc.ts` and writes the total onto `estimator_estimate_lines.qty`, so nothing downstream changes; a measured line's quantity is read-only. Rows are draft-only in the database and stand after Make official as the record of where the quantity came from. Rows **copy to another work** of the estimate (a wall's rows feed masonry, plaster and paint) and **Duplicate** copies a row within its own sheet — both read from the database, never from the browser.
7. **Units are picked from the shared Masters list** (`0082`), and a work's unit is **refused**, not warned, while any estimate line uses it — 40 cum becoming 40 sqm is the same number describing a different building.
8. **The estimate reads as a BOQ**: one grand total, works grouped by category with subtotals, one rate and one amount per line. `groupLineCosts` in `calc.ts` produces it; anything that prints an estimate renders its output rather than grouping again.
9. **Site check goes villa by villa** (founder, 2026-10-16: "a villa wise list you click in and see"): a card per villa with an official estimate, counting what waits for approval, what is past the estimate and what was approved outside it — waiting first (`villaSummaries`). A villa (`/estimator/site-check/[unitId]`) holds its over-estimate and outside-the-estimate rows with Approve on the row, and every material estimated against **reached site** — store issues to the villa plus deliveries straight to it. Each material across all villas is one tap away (`/materials`). All from `getOfficialComparisons` and `site-check.ts`. An approval **carries to the villa's later officials**, matched on (work, item) — read-side only. An outside row shows **site's reason** from the supervisor's request (`issue_requests.off_estimate_reason`, matched on plot, work and item) — a read of Supervisors' table that `0110` admits `/estimator` to.
10. **No scheduling** — "this whole build is only estimation". If it is ever built, Relay's missing per-activity dates are where it goes.
11. **An indent line outside the estimate waits for the estimator** (`0113`, founder 2026-10-11: "the first thing reconciled in the estimate … indent should wait for estimator approval for sure"). Site check opens with **Indents waiting for you**: each submitted indent's lines no estimate or budget covers, with site's reason, **Approve** (one press) and **Refuse** (a note the approver reads). The indent cannot be approved until every one is approved; an answer can be changed while the indent waits. Written through `decide_indent_line()` onto Indents' table — the cross-tool write in `SECURITY.md`; read from Indents' open tables (`lib/estimator/indent-queries.ts`). Approving is not adding: **putting the material on the estimate** is the villa's working estimate (the row links to it) and Make official, after which the next indent pulls it from there. An approval at the indent **counts in Site check** when that material reaches the villa, matched on (villa, the indent's work, item) — read-side, nothing copied into `estimator_reconciliation_approvals`; an indent naming no work matches nothing there. The welcome screen counts the lines waiting.

12. **A SketchUp model measures a villa** (`0118`, founder 2026-10-14: "a hand off system … in one click to go the tool box as an estimate"). Goodearth QS for SketchUp (built outside this repo, `Documents\3Dqs_Sandbox\goodearth-qs`) opens **From the model** (`/estimator/from-model`) in SketchUp's own window and hands it the takeoff; elsewhere the page takes the saved file. `lib/estimator/model-takeoff.ts` is the contract — the file's shape, read and checked, and what an import would do. The founder's answers (2026-10-15):
    - **Into the villa's working estimate only**; with none, Import starts a blank one. Works match on an active `work_items.code`, and **the unit must equal the rate book's or that work is refused** — nothing converts (decision 7). The rest of the file still imports.
    - **Model rows are the model's** (`from_model`, `model_refs`): `import_model_rows()` replaces the estimate's model rows whole, in one transaction, and never touches a typed row; a line the model measured alone and no longer measures goes back to _to measure_. Edit, remove and duplicate refuse a model row — it changes in the model and is sent again. _Copy to another work_ makes it a typed row.
    - **Works the rate book measures by hand** go on as _to measure_. Every model row passes the typed row's check (`measurement-rules.ts`), and the quantity comes from `calc.ts` through the same `syncLineQty` (`sheet-sync.ts`) — SQL never sums.
    - **The estimate remembers its model** (file, SketchUp's id, when, who); the preview warns before a different model lands on the villa, and before the model's rows replace a typed quantity.
    - **The rate book says how the model measures each work** (`estimator_work_info.model_measure`; blank follows the unit). **Download works list for SketchUp** (or _Send the works list to SketchUp_ inside its window) is how the model learns it. `0118` and `scripts/import-masters-workbook.ts` set FD.03.04 (Footing) to base and sides, and FD.03.05, FD.04.04, FD.04.11–14 and every package work to by hand, wherever blank. **The codes changed on 2026-10-22** (FD.15 is FD.03.04): a model tagged with an old code is refused as "not in the toolbox's works" until it is re-tagged; send the works list again first.
    - **Anyone holding `/estimator` imports.** The file is untrusted input, read again on the server before anything is written; nothing in it decides identity or access.
13. **Budget against actual, villa by villa** (`0136`, founder 2026-10-22: "visible to estimator and bills holders"). `/estimator/villas/[unitId]/budget`, opened from Site check and from any villa's bill: per stage and work, the official estimate (labour and material apart), what is committed (issued POs bought for the villa's indents, approved work orders) and what is actual (material reached at its batch rates, labour billed), and what is left. Before GST; material billed is never added to actual, because the PO already counts. A part with no rate reads "+ unknown", never a smaller whole. All from the money view `villa_cost_facts` (gated in its own `WHERE` to `/estimator`, `/bills`, `/reporter`) and `lib/estimator/budget-vs-actual.ts` (pure, tested). **It is the one Estimator page a `/bills` holder opens**: it sits outside the `(tool)` route group, whose layout admits `/estimator` alone, and `requireBudgetReader()` admits either grant; the official estimate's number and its link show to `/estimator` only.

## The rules everything rests on

- **Every table here is `/estimator`-gated, SELECT included** (`0074`). Masters reads are ungated, so a rate cannot live there — a work's unit and labour rate are in `estimator_work_info`, never on `work_items`. It reads Inventory's money-free `stock_issues(_lines)` and `goods_receipts(_lines)`; other tools see it only through its three views (`SECURITY.md`), and the two open ones carry no rate, ever.
- **A missing rate is `null`, and `null` is never zero.** The calculator returns `null` for any cost it cannot know, `formatMoney(null)` prints "—", and a frozen unpriced figure stays unpriced forever (BUGCATCHER #13).
- **All arithmetic is in `lib/estimator/calc.ts`**, pure and tested. The rate book's cost per unit and an estimate line's cost come from the same `computeLine`.
- **Costs are live while working, frozen at official** (`0077`). The app computes the snapshot — per-row costs and the per-(work, material) takeoff — and SQL validates it, never re-implements it. **The takeoff stays one row per work and material** however many rows a work has, so Indents, Inventory and Supervisors read what they always did; `estimate_work_facts` adds a work's rows up. An official work with several rows shows each row's labour and its own material cost, not a per-row material list.
- **One working and one official estimate per villa** (partial unique indexes); a race loses at the index, loudly. Only drafts are edited or deleted (`delete_draft_estimate`, one transaction). A template is an estimate with no villa (`check (is_template = (unit_id is null))`); no screen makes one.
- **Deletion is refused, not cascaded** — a material used in a recipe is deactivated, never deleted.
- **Material outside the official estimate is flagged forever** (`0083`). An approval given at a stage indent (`0158`) covers the item on every active work of that stage. Detection is derived, per (work, item), from issues and direct-to-site deliveries; only the estimator's acknowledgement is stored, with no delete policy and no un-approve. The amber badge never clears.
- **No embeds** — `units` has two paths to `plots`, so names merge through a `Map` (BUGCATCHER #2).

## Things that will bite

- **An official must not point back at the working estimate it came from.** `source_estimate_id` is ON DELETE SET NULL and a submitted header refuses updates, so deleting the working estimate would fail on the cascade. `make_estimate_official` leaves it null on purpose; a "made from" link needs its own column.
- **Nothing converts units** — not the recipes (quantities are per one unit of the parent, and screens print "bags per cum") and not the measurement sheet (feet on a cum work gives a confident wrong number; the unit beside the total is the only guard). Nothing stops Masters holding two units that mean the same thing.
- **A mix with nothing in it contributes nothing** rather than erroring; the "nothing in it yet" flag is all that stands between an empty mix and a quietly cheap estimate.
- **A copy pairs rows by `copied_from`, never by work** (`0157`): two rows of one work would otherwise swap their materials, measurements and frozen costs. `copied_from` has no foreign key on purpose — a working row is deleted while its official copy stays frozen. Rows within a work keep their order by `created_at`, which the copy carries over.
- **The model fills one row per work**: the row it measured last time, else the first unlabelled one, else the first (`import_model_rows` and `planImport` agree). Two rows of a work can also come from two adds racing — visible, as both show the name box, but not refused.
- **`estimator_copy_estimate_contents` must carry `from_model` and `model_refs`** (`0118` asserts it). A copy that drops them turns model rows into typed rows in an official or a villa started from another, and the next send counts them twice.
- **SketchUp's window keeps its own sign-in, and login doesn't return to the page asked for**: the first time, sign in, then press Send again. A `next=` for exactly this path would need `SECURITY.md`'s argument first.
- **A unit changed in the rate book after SketchUp loaded its works list is refused on import** — the fix is sending the list again, never a conversion. `window.sketchup` exists only inside SketchUp, and the page trusts it for nothing beyond "here is a takeoff".
- **`model_refs` are SketchUp element ids**: they survive saving and copying the file, not exploding and regrouping — for tracing a row back, never for matching.
- **Cells still refresh the page on save.** Skipping it (Budgets' `saveLine` precedent) needs the whole BOQ computed in the browser — worth doing once the rework has settled.
