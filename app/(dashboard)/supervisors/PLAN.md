# Supervisors — the rules

A phone-first app for site supervisors: log the day's labour, see what material each work has drawn against the official estimate, see the released drawings, and request store issues. Grant `/supervisors`. Migrations `0084`, `0104`, `0132`, `0134`, `0135`, `0137`, `0138`.

## What it is

- **Villa picker** — every supervisor sees every villa (founder, 2026-08-20; per-supervisor plots is an open question in `TODO.md`). The working anchor is the **plot**, like stock issues; the unit is found through the 1:1 when the estimate is needed.
- **Labour logs** — one row per plot + work + contractor + date + kind (`0104`), in the billing team's three formats: **daily wages** (heads by trade — masons / helpers / others), **piece-work by quantity** (how much was done, in the work's unit from the rate book through the money-free `work_unit_facts` — the unit is read on the server, never typed on the phone), or **piece-work, lump sum** (what was done, in words). Edited, not accumulated: a second entry for the same day, kind and **work class** edits the first. **Every entry says what it was for** (`work_class`, `0132`, founder 2026-10-22): standard work, additional work or rework; billed, the class is frozen with the rest, and Send to Bill shows it beside the work. The shapes are `lib/supervisors/labour.ts` (pure, tested) and the database's CHECK. **Quantities, never wages** — the billing team prices them in Bills (founder 2026-10-08, reversing "heads, never wages" for piece-work only).
- **A billed log is frozen** — Bills' Send to Bill stamps `bill_id` (`0106`); the guard then refuses any edit or delete, and the villa page shows "Billed · BILL/…" (from the money-free `bill_facts`) instead of Edit and Delete.
- **Requests for issue** — plot, work, item, quantity in the item's unit, and the work class (`0132`), which the store's Fulfil carries onto the issue and resolving can never rewrite; `requested → fulfilled / declined`. The supervisor edits or withdraws an open request; the store-keeper resolves it (`inventory/PLAN.md`); a resolved request is history. The form quick-picks from the official estimate's materials for the work, with the whole catalogue as fallback. **Something off the estimate needs a reason** (`0104`, founder: "allowed with a reason"): when the villa has an official estimate and it doesn't list that material for that work, the form asks "Why is it needed?" and the action refuses without one — decided on the server from `estimate_takeoff_facts`, never from the phone. The reason is permanent with the request, and the Estimator's Site check shows it.
- **Deliveries to this villa** (`0135`) — the villa's newest store issues and deliveries straight to site, each confirmed once by the supervisor: quantities start at what was sent, a short line needs a reason, never more than was sent (`lib/supervisors/site-receipt.ts`, pure, tested; the database checks the line and the quantity). The confirmation is this tool's own append-only record (`site_receipt_confirmations(_lines)`, read by `/inventory` for the issue and delivery notes) — never a write into Inventory's tables. Its header and lines are two inserts, so a confirmation whose lines did not all land is finished by the next press.
- **Site checklists** (`0134`, the operations team's workbook) — templates (`checklist_templates`, `checklist_template_items`) read by anyone signed in and kept by `/supervisors` under Supervisors → Checklists, loaded once by `scripts/import-checklists.ts` (`lib/supervisors/checklist-workbook.ts`, pure, tested): the ten inspection checklists and the concrete grade record, whose items are works answered with the grade in Remarks. On the villa page a supervisor starts one (a row per active check), answers each Pass, Fail or N/A with a remark, attaches one file (a test certificate or a photo, private `site-checklists` bucket), and signs off. Sign-off needs every check answered and a remark on each Fail and N/A; signed off, it never changes; only an open one can be deleted (`site_checklists_guard`, mirrored in `lib/supervisors/checklists.ts`). Amber "to confirm" notes are the workbook's technical-review items, shown as hints. **A check is never reworded** — signed-off checklists point at it — so a changed check is the old one switched off and a new one added; templates and checks are switched off, never deleted.
- **The checklist runner** (plan.md phase C) — an open checklist opens one check per screen: the check in large type, Pass / Fail / N/A as big buttons; Pass saves and moves to the next unanswered check, Fail and N/A ask for the remark first, so a runner-answered checklist never stalls at sign-off; "All clear" then Sign off. **A photo per check** (`site_checklist_items.photo_path`, the same private bucket, served by id through `/supervisors/checklists/photos/[itemId]`), replaced by a new one, frozen at sign-off by the item guard. The list view (`?view=list`) keeps the details, the attachment and every answer. **The stages a checklist inspects** are set under Supervisors → Checklists → Stages.
- **Drawings** — released revisions per work, through the shared `lib/drawings/` (`design-management/PLAN.md`).
- **The programme** (`0137`, founder 2026-10-23) — a villa moves through **stages, a stage being a work group** (`SS.01 Block work up to GF lintel`), in **the site sequence's order** (`site_sequence`, one list for every villa, not the works list's order: slab conduiting comes before the slab). A villa's programme is the sequence's stages its official estimate covers (every one without an official estimate), plus any other stage the estimate covers or something happened on — derived on every read (`lib/supervisors/programme.ts`, pure, tested); a `site_stages` row exists only once something happened on that stage. Status is not started → working → done, with **waiting** (on material, labour, an inspection, the client or something else) beside them; the database keeps the dates in line with the status, and `site_stage_events` is the history only its triggers write. **The first labour logged on a stage starts it** (`labour_logs_start_stage`, as the caller — only `/supervisors` writes labour logs, and Bills' stamp moves only `bill_id`; an edit fires it only when the work changes). A tap saves at once with Undo, which puts back the time the stage entered its old status too — the database takes that time only when the stage's history records it (`0138`), and otherwise times every change itself; **Done with an unsigned checklist asks once and never blocks**. **Health**, first match wins: waiting more than 3 days · past its planned finish · no labour on a working stage for 5 days · an open checklist with a failed check · a delivery unconfirmed for 2 days · on track. **One supervisor per villa** (`site_villas`) is the board's Mine filter, never a permission. **Checklists belong to stages** through `checklist_template_groups`, and a checklist started from a stage names it. The page reads the estimate's work quantities through the money-free `estimate_work_facts`, so piece-work logged reads "640 of 1,800 sqft".
- **The schedule** (plan.md phase B, no migration of its own) — **every date is worked out on read, never stored** (`lib/supervisors/schedule.ts`, pure, tested); the only typed inputs are a villa's start date, the sequence, and a stage's own days or "not before" date. **The sequence** (Supervisors → Sequence, one list for every villa): each stage follows another, **after** it finishes or **alongside** it, plus **wait days** for curing, and has **typical days**. A stage following one the villa does not have follows what that one followed (zero length, no wait of its own); one the sequence does not name follows the stage before it; taking a stage out re-points its followers the same way (to what it followed, after or alongside as it was), so the dates match a villa that simply lacks it. **A stage's days**, first that applies: the villa's own → **learned** once 3 villas have finished it (the middle days per sqft × this villa's sqft when sizes are known, else the middle days) → typical days × villa sqft ÷ `typical_sqft` (unscaled when either size is missing) → none, and nothing after it has dates. A villa's sqft is its official estimate's `SS.01.00` quantity. A finished stage dated to the single day it was tapped Done is not learned from — that is a catch-up tap, not a length. A stage row holding only days or a first plan never keeps a stage in the programme. **Calendar days**, both ends counted: a 5-day stage from the 1st finishes on the 5th, and the next starts on the 6th. **The forecast**: done = its real dates; working or waiting = its start + days, or today if later (running long pushes everything after it); not started = the latest of its chain, its "not before" date and today — a "not before" the chain has overtaken says so. **The first plan** copies today's forecast (worked out on the server, never sent from the phone) into each stage's `baseline_start/finish` and stamps `baselined_at`; setting it again asks why, and the history keeps the note; each stage shows its slip in the Programme, and a stage that joined later is left out of the first plan's handover. Stage rows made only to hold a plan don't show in the history. Health gains **past its planned finish** (the first plan's finish, else start + days), after a long wait and before no labour.

## Settled decisions

- **`issue_requests` is Supervisors-owned; Inventory's fulfil/decline is a documented cross-tool write** (`SECURITY.md`). RLS admits both apps on SELECT and UPDATE (and `/estimator` on SELECT, for Site check — `0110`), one policy per verb, and `issue_requests_guard()` holds the fine grain: identity permanent, content edits only while `requested` and only by `/supervisors`, transitions only by `/inventory`, `fulfilled` needs its issue id, `declined` needs a reason.
- **The materials view derives, never stores** — `groupSiteMaterials` (`lib/supervisors/site-materials.ts`, pure, tested) sets drawn quantities against `estimate_takeoff_facts` per work. It restates the house conversion rule (factor, matching labels 1:1, else raw and labelled) rather than importing the Estimator's `compare.ts` — one tool never imports another's code. Narrowing filters over that view must follow its schema (BUGCATCHER #16).
- **Contractors are vendors** with `is_contractor`; `labour_logs_contractor_only` refuses any other vendor id at the database.
- **No money anywhere** — `labour_logs` is `/supervisors` on every verb (Bills also reads it, for Send to Bill), and `estimate_takeoff_facts` and `work_unit_facts` carry no rate, ever (pinned in the view manifest).
