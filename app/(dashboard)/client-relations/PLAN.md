# Client Relations — the rules

The Saarang plot register, one record per villa; every promise to a client as an action with an owner and a date; and **the only money coming in** anywhere in the app — a payment schedule with receipts against it. Grant `/client-relations`, and a team lead tick beside it (`0122`). Migrations `0050`, `0051`, `0120`, `0121`, `0123` (and Masters' `0119`, the build progress list).

## The founder's decisions

1. **One list.** A prospect and a client are the same record at different stages; "added to the master" is the moment they are given a plot, so `clients` grew a lifecycle instead of a parallel table.
2. **A milestone schedule, not a single agreement value**, so "overdue" is answerable. Plot payment is worked out from it, never typed — the Saarang tracker's "Completed / Pending – registration" word came in as a note.
3. **Fixed vocabularies** for the sales and legal columns, so they can be counted and filtered. Since `0120` they are the Saarang tracker's lists (deed and CA: not started, drafting, not signed, signed; the original with client, bank, Chess Cafe, Sreejayan or Tarana; five acknowledgements; seven bottlenecks; seven interior stages).
4. **Design status from Relay — amended 2026-10-16** ("forget relay for this build do as needed"): Relay knows whole trails, never "ground floor slab", so the team now types **build progress** and the three **design checks** (client confirmed design, permit, working drawing). Relay's trails still show on a plot's page, read as they are.
5. **The Saarang tracker is the flow** (2026-10-16: "study this ui thats the flow they would love so integrate accordingly and edit the current tool to match the vibe"). The self-hosted tracker the CRM team used became this tool's Actions screen, Plots grid and plot page; every other screen took its look. Its paint did not: the toolbox's tones, Geist and its one green (DESIGN.md).
6. **Each person sees the work assigned to them — amended 2026-10-17** ("only those who are assigned a task or own a deal get to see the work except for admins"; asked who else: "admin and team lead"). A **plot** is yours if you are its Construction or Interior owner (decision 8), or own or set an open action on it; its payment schedule and receipts go with it. An **action** is yours if you own it, set it, or it is on your plot. A plot nobody owns is for admins and team leads until it is given out. Anyone who sees a plot or an action can still hand it to a colleague, and stops seeing it. **Admins and the tool's team leads** (Settings, beside the grant) see everything and open on the team view; a member opens on their own list, with a switch to everything they can see. The database holds the line (`0123`; SECURITY.md, _Assigned work_) — the screens only follow it.
7. **Build progress is a Masters list** ("construction stage lets make a masters table that lists it and then link it here") — `progress_stages`, beside, and not to be confused with, construction stages (Masters' `PLAN.md`).
8. **A plot has two sides, Construction and Interior** (2026-10-17: "some people may get the interiors or construction of the same plot so it needs to basically maybe be assigned twice and treated a bit differently … call it interior and construction kaadal can be avoided"). Each side has its own owner and its own actions. Both owners see the whole plot, as does everyone else who can see it (asked: "the whole plot"; the Construction owner sees "all of the Interior side"). The plot shows both owners; Next steps, Actions and Plots show both sides or one (`?side=construction|interior`); a new step on a plot goes to its side's owner; Interior lists only plots with interior work (`plotInList`). "Kaadal" no longer appears on these screens.

## The rules everything rests on

1. **The grain is the plot; the list is client-first.** `client_engagements` hangs off `unit_id`; people are found by name. A client with two villas gets two lot cards.
2. **No `client_id` on an engagement** — the client is `units.client_id`; a second copy would drift.
3. **"Signed, Bank Original" is two columns**, so "sale deed signed" is one equality. A CHECK refuses a custodian on an unsigned deed; moving a deed off signed clears its custodian in the same write, so a chip never meets a constraint name.
4. **The nine payment stages are seeded; the invoice stage is derived** — the furthest rung with a date, never a stored position that could disagree with its rows.
5. **Each plot's dues are its own ledger.** An unallocated receipt spills into the oldest unpaid rung, so roll-ups add the answers, never the inputs (`combineSummaries`, tested) — or Villa 17's money would settle Villa 39's instalment.
6. **The whole tool is gated, SELECT included.** Money is one reason; `details`, and now every action's words and notes, are the stronger. Reporter and Financial Management read owner views whose **omission of the prose columns is the boundary** — actions never join a facts view — and since `0123` those views admit no Client Relations member, who would otherwise read every plot's money there.
7. **Dates compare as ISO strings with `today` passed in.** `todayInIndia()` (and `hourInIndia()` for the greeting) are the only clock reads, and nothing pure calls them.
8. **The tracker's rules live in `lib/client-relations/next-steps.ts`, pure and tested.** **No date is overdue** — an action without one sits with the overdue until someone gives it a real date. **Waiting on management outranks everything.** A sold plot with nothing open has **no next step**, unless it is handed over. **Not updated** is two weeks without a change to the plot or any of its actions.
9. **An action is never deleted** — `client_actions` has no DELETE policy; it is marked done, with Undo. `done_on` moves with the status in one CHECK. Any date a person picks clears _date assumed_.
10. **An action's owner is on the team** — `client_relations_team()`: active, non-admin people holding the grant. Every write checks it, so a step is never given to someone who could not see it. Admins are the managers: they clear "waiting on management" rather than own the list. Team leads are on the team and see the team view, but closing anyone's action stays an admin's (rule 13).
11. **Chips save when tapped; text saves when you leave the box.** One field per write (`updatePlot`, `updateNextStep`), the tracker's PATCH — there is no form to half-fill.
12. **Build progress reads its meaning from Masters' order**: the first stage is the paperwork before ground is broken (the rail's build step starts filling from the second); the last active stage is the handover.
13. **Only whoever set an action, or a manager, closes it** (`0121`; founder 2026-10-16: "you can only complete an action that you set, not everyone can"). Its owner's tick marks it **finished — waiting to confirm**, which tops the setter's list until they tick it closed or send it back; anyone else's tick is locked. Opening a closed action again is the setter's or a manager's too. Every other change — words, date, owner, plot — stays open to the whole grant. `closeRight` in `next-steps.ts` says who may do what; the `client_actions_guard` trigger holds the same line in the database.
14. **A member's Clients list is their own clients** — the ones they look after and the buyers of their plots (`clientScope`). The client record itself is Masters' shared list, readable by everyone signed in; what `0123` locks is the CRM's work on it. A new prospect is handled by whoever adds it, unless they pick someone else.
15. **Selling an unsold plot gives it a Construction owner** — the client's handler if they are on the team, else the seller (`crm_assign_unit`, `0123`) — so it never vanishes from the seller's list as they sell it. A plot with a buyer changes hands only through someone who can see it.

## Things that will bite

- **An embed through `units` to `plots` names the key** (`plots!units_plot_id_fkey`, BUGCATCHER #2).
- **The stored names are the tracker's; the screens' words are not** (decision 8). An action's `track` `sales` is Construction and `kaadal` is Interior; `crm_owner_id` is the Construction owner, and `kaadal_owner_id`, `kaadal_stage` and `kaadal_note` are the Interior side. `stages.ts` holds the words; a URL says `construction` or `interior` (`trackForSide`), never a stored value.
- **`client_engagements` has four FKs to `profiles`** (`crm_owner_id`, `kaadal_owner_id`, `created_by`, `updated_by`) and `client_actions` three: any embed of `profiles` through either names its key. The reads use a name map instead.
- **`crm_assign_unit`, `crm_release_unit`, `client_relations_team()` and the four visibility helpers (`crm_owns_plot`, `crm_sees_plot`, `crm_sees_action`, `crm_may_place_action`, `0123`) are `security definer`** — the `has_app` check in each body IS the boundary. Smoke-test them as a no-grant account and as a member (`scripts/trials/client-relations-scope.sql`).
- **`create_client_engagement`** may not be executed by any client role, and its body admits `/client-relations` **or** a call through the `units_seed_engagement` trigger — its real caller, firing for a `/masters` user. A plain `has_app` check would break adding a plot in Masters (BUGCATCHER #11).
- **It reads `pusher_chain_state` directly**, never `lib/relay/queries.ts` (every function there requires `/relay`). The small staff-name map is duplicated on purpose.
- **Two functions, not one with a null** (`0051`): the type generator cannot express a nullable uuid argument.
- **The bottleneck array needs two CHECKs** (`<@` allows duplicates and a NULL element), and `normaliseBottlenecks` dedupes and sorts before every write.
- **A plot's page sends its cards a slim set of fields** (`plotFields`), not the whole record — six client cards would otherwise each carry its actions, payments and Relay trails to the browser.
- **The board's shared data rides in one context** (`BoardProvider`): today, the team, every plot, each person's open count — handed down once, not repeated on every action card.
- **The import keys each action to `md5('saarang-tracker:<id>')`** — a second run adds nothing; never reuse that prefix for anything else.
- **Every read answers with what the viewer may see** (`0123`), so on a member's screen every count is a count of their own. A colleague's open count is shown only to an admin or a lead (`seesAll` on the board); to a member it would be a wrong number that looks right.
- **Two flags, not one**: `isManager` (an admin — closes anyone's action, `0121`) and `seesAll` (an admin or a lead — the team view, `isToolLead`). A lead given the close tick would meet the database's refusal.
- **The action read policy asks by id after the row's own columns** (`crm_sees_action`). Asked over the columns alone, Postgres's check of the updated row refuses a hand-over; without the columns branch, an insert that asks for its row back (`.insert().select()`) is refused — the row is not there yet to be found by id.
- **A plot or client a member cannot see renders `NotYours`**, not a 404 — including the plot page that refreshes after its owner hands it on.
- **`crm_assign_unit` sets an unsold plot's owner inside the function**: the seller cannot update a plot record they cannot see yet.
- **`created_by` is the setter, and it is pinned** (`0121`): on insert it is whoever is signed in, whatever the request sent; a script's insert (no session) pins the owner; afterwards it never changes. Without the pin, an action with no setter could be handed to yourself and then closed. The guard lets a session with no `auth.uid()` (db:apply, the import scripts) through.

## Known costs, accepted

- **No live refresh.** The tracker polled every 20 seconds; here a colleague's change shows on the next click, as in every tool.
- **Saarang West's configurations live in its villas' names** ("Villa 3 (1BHK Study)"), because Masters' unit type is only villa, apartment or duplex; the plot cards read it back from the name.
- **The Relay panel is empty for most plots** until trails are filed; the build stage no longer waits on it.
- **A closed action on a colleague's plot reads "A colleague's plot"** to its setter: the plot left their sight when the action closed.
- **Holding one open action on a plot opens the whole plot record** — its notes and payments too. The founder's rule; narrower would need a second, slimmer plot view.
- **Owner changes are open to anyone who sees the plot**, as before `0123` — so someone holding an action on a plot can make themselves its Construction or Interior owner. Restricting owner changes to leads is one migration away, if wanted.
- **Most clients have a name and nothing else.** Blank is not a failed read. Joint names are one client; no co-applicant model without asking.
- **The import fills blanks only**, and leaves alone any plot whose buyer the tracker and Masters disagree on. On 2026-10-16 the founder ruled the tracker right ("some buyers cancel some new buyers have come"), and `scripts/update-saarang-register.ts` brought Masters in line first: cancelled buyers marked lost with the reason, new buyers assigned, Plots 33 and 34 made one, Saarang West's plots added.
