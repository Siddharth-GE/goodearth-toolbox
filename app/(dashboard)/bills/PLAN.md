# Bills — the rules

Grant `/bills`. Migrations `0025`, `0026`, `0104`–`0107`, `0111`, `0133`, `0136`, `0156`.

The accounts-facing record of what Goodearth owes and has paid. A bill is one of **three kinds** — against an issued purchase order, against an **approved** work order, or **labour with no work order** (kind `nmr`: miscellaneous work or a muster roll, its contractor optional) with no anchor at all — numbered `BILL/<project>/<plot-or-unit-or-GEN>/NNN` — or, when it is a contractor's labour bill, `<contractor code>-<PW|NMR>-<company code>-NN` (12 below) — moving recorded → approved → paid, with send-back carrying a mandatory note.

**A bill has lines** (`0106`, founder 2026-10-08 — reversing 0025's "a bill has no line items": "2 cum at ₹4,000 should show ₹8,000"). A line is a material from the bill's PO, daily wages by trade, piece-work by quantity or lump sum, or anything else; its money is the shared line formula (`lib/line-money.ts`, as a PO line). **The header follows the lines**: `bill_lines_roll_up` rewrites taxable, GST and total from them to the paisa, so every money view keeps reading the header. A bill from before lines keeps its typed figures (and can be given lines while recorded). The rules are `lib/bills/lines.ts` and `lib/bills/math.ts` (pure, tested).

## The rules everything rests on

1. **The status machine lives in the database** (`bills_guard`): recorded → approved → paid, send-back approved → recorded with a mandatory note that the next approval clears. Header and amount edits only while recorded; anchor, vendor, scope and number permanent. `lib/bills/workflow.ts` mirrors it **for buttons only**.
2. **Approvers are a named list** (`bill_approvers`, managed in **Settings** beside indent approvers; admins always may). **Self-approval is allowed** — founder decision. The same list approves work orders and releases the weekly cash request.
3. **Amounts are the lines' sums** — on screen the header is read-only once a bill has lines; each line's quantity, rate, GST, discount (₹) and other charges are edited to match the paper. **Additional charges** on the whole bill (`0133`: an amount, its own GST % and what for, on a bill with lines) join taxable and GST in the roll-up — `bills_charges_roll_up` re-sums the header when only the charges change; the screen and the print share `summaryRows`. **A daily-wages bill's total may be set by hand** when the muster roll differs, with `total_override_note` saying why; the roll-up then leaves the total alone. A **material bill starts from its PO** — the order is picked first and brings its vendor; then **one delivery** may be picked, and its supplier invoice (`goods_receipts.invoice_no`, `0132`) and what it brought fill the bill (a delivery already billed, or with nothing left, is not offered), else everything received and not yet billed (`po_line_billing_facts`: received − already billed, the PO's rate and GST, a % discount turned to rupees, rupee discount and charges shared by quantity); a **work-order bill from its works**. **A material line is never billed past what arrived** (`bill_lines_guard`, `0156`, checked only when a quantity rises; the actions check first, so nothing half-saves). Over-billing against the PO value or work order value **warns, never blocks**.
4. **Money is gated** — every Bills table is `/bills` (plus `/reporter`) on SELECT, and the windows in and out are listed in `SECURITY.md`, _Money stays confined_. Others see bills only through the money-free `bill_facts`.
5. **The mint derives everything from the anchor.** `create_bill()` reads the money-free `po_facts` — a `/bills`-only user cannot read the PO tables — or the contract row, copies project/plot/unit/vendor, resolves the scope, and mints via `bill_counters`. **Deliberately no vendor-inactive check**: a real invoice from a deactivated vendor still enters the books, and the contract's own `is_active` is the off-switch.
6. **Work orders belong to Bills**, not Masters (`0026` moved the labour contracts here on the founder's correction; `0105` made them work orders, founder 2026-10-08). **The table is still `labour_contracts`** — renamed on every screen only, so `bills.kind = 'contract'`, `labour_contract_id`, Financial Management and Reporter are untouched. Made at `/bills/work-orders` by any `/bills` holder: a contractor (a vendor marked as a contractor), the project and villa, what it covers, **its works** (`labour_contract_lines` — a work from the Masters list at the rate book's unit and labour rate through the gated `work_labour_rate_facts`, or a lump sum) and **its terms** (copied from the default `work_order` text in Masters → Terms). Numbered `WO/<project>/NNN` by the database on insert (`wo_counters`; needs the project's short code). `pending_approval` until a bill approver approves (their approval limit applies); while it has works its value **is their sum** — a trigger writes it — and works, value and terms are permanent once approved. Correcting an approved order is switching it off and making a new one. **Going** (`0144`): an order waiting for approval is deleted with its works (`delete_pending_work_order`); an approved one is **cancelled** with a reason by a bill approver or admin while no bill, advance or cash request line names it (an advance reversed in full no longer counts, `0147`), and is switched off once one does. A cancelled order is final, listed only under Cancelled, and every picker and `villa_cost_facts` read approved orders only; a new bill, advance or cash request line takes a share lock on its order (`work_order_not_cancelled`), so it can never land on one cancelled at the same moment. Any `/bills` holder deletes a pending order or a draft cash request; a cancel ignores the approver's limit, because it authorises no spending. **Paid by stage** (`0136`, Superstructure and MEP): "Add every package work" puts one line per package of the stage — the works its rate book prices per sqft (`packageLines`) — at the villa's built-up sqft. A bill line names the work order line it pays (`bill_lines.labour_contract_line_id`, checked by `bill_lines_guard` to be on the bill's own order); `work_order_line_billing_facts` (money, `/bills` or `/reporter`) gives each line's value and what is billed against it before GST, the order's page shows billed and left per line, and a bill from the order starts each line at what is left (`linesFromWorkOrder`), a fully billed line left out. Over-billing still warns, never blocks. **Templates** (`work_order_templates`): "Save as template" keeps an order's works and terms without quantities or rates; "Start from a template" fills a new one; switched off, never deleted. The rules are `lib/bills/work-orders.ts` (pure, tested); the print is `lib/bills/work-order-document.tsx`. `kind` on bills is explicit (`po`/`contract`/`nmr`) with CHECKs tying it to the anchors; `vendor_id` is nullable **for NMR only** — a contractor when one supplied the workers, nothing when the muster roll is paid directly.

7. **Send to Bill** (`/bills/labour`): the billing team ticks the labour entries the supervisors logged (one kind, one contractor, one project) and `send_labour_logs_to_bill()` makes the bill, its lines and stamps every entry — all or nothing. Daily wages: heads × a day rate per trade (the contractor's last bill's rates suggested). Piece-work: against the contractor's approved work order when one is picked, each entry's rate offered from the order's line for that work, else the rate book; with no work order (`0133`, founder 2026-10-22) it is a direct labour bill — kind `nmr`, the contractor as vendor, the same piece-work lines. A stamped entry is frozen on Supervisors' side; on the bill its lines and quantities are fixed (only the money may change); **deleting a recorded bill goes through `delete_recorded_bill()`**, which sets its entries free to be sent again. Rules: `lib/bills/labour-billing.ts` (pure, tested).
8. **Every bill prints** (`/bills/[id]/pdf`, `lib/bills/bill-document.tsx`): the project's company, the number with the vendor's name beside it, lines, totals, paid and pending; DRAFT until approved.
9. **Paid is what the money says** (`0107`, founder 2026-10-08). Money goes out only against an **approved** bill: payments (part or whole, each with its UTR / cheque / UPI reference) and **advance recoveries** (part of an advance set against one of the same vendor's bills; an advance goes to a contractor or a material supplier, founder 2026-10-22). Neither takes a bill past its total, an advance is recovered only from the same contractor's bills and never past what is left of it, and when the two reach the total the database marks the bill **paid** by itself — there is no "Mark paid". Money is append-only: no update or delete on payments, advances or recoveries. **A mistake is reversed** (`0147`): a bill approver or an admin presses Reverse, with a reason, on the bill or on Payments, and `reverse_bill_money` enters the same amount the other way as a second row pointing at the first (`reverses_id`), copying its bill, advance and reference — `money_reversal_guard` re-reads them, so a direct insert fakes nothing. Every sum nets by itself, so totals, pending and the cash request's paid all follow with no filter. Once per row, never a reversal of a reversal; an advance only once nothing is left recovered from it (reverse the recoveries first), and nothing is recovered from a reversed advance. A paid bill that falls below its total goes back to approved, and a bill can be sent back only while its money nets to nothing — so a bill paid in error is reversed, sent back, corrected and approved again. Its rows still point at it, so it is never deleted: Delete says so, and its payments stay on its page. The rules on screen are `moneyReversal` in `lib/bills/ledger.ts`; the trial is `scripts/trials/payment-reversals.sql`. A bill already paid before `0107` was given one payment of its total on its `paid_at` (idempotent on `bill_id`), so history reads the same way.
10. **The weekly cash request** (`/bills/cash-requests`): accounts adds approved bills with something pending (asking for all or part) and advances (a contractor, a project, optionally a work order) → submits → a **bill approver** releases it, cutting any line by lowering what is released (lines not cut go as asked), or sends it back with a note → each released line is paid (a bill payment, or the advance given) → closed. A draft is deleted with its lines (`delete_draft_cash_request`, `0144`); a submitted one comes back to draft through its approver first. **An approved bill is never cancelled:** while no money is out an approver sends it back, and a recorded bill is deleted. **Payments** (`/bills/payments`) lists every payment, advance and recovery with the A4 bar; **Contractors** (`/bills/contractors`) is the advance summary: billed, paid, pending; advances given, recovered, still out. Rules: `lib/bills/ledger.ts` and `math.ts` (pure, tested); the founder's "₹25 lakh billed, ₹20 lakh released, ₹5 lakh pending" is a test.

11. **Contractors** (`0156`, team's additions 2026-10-26): a contractor gets **CON/001…** from a sequence when marked one in Masters, kept for good; **every bill takes its vendor's own count** (`bills.vendor_seq` from `bill_vendor_counters`, never reused), printed as "Their bill no.". **Bills → Contractors** lists contractors (or suppliers, or everyone) by company and project; a name opens **its ledger** — every approved bill, payment, advance and recovery by date with what Goodearth owes after each (`contractorLedger`, `lib/bills/ledger.ts`): a recovery moves nothing unless its advance or bill is outside the project or company shown. **An advance is suggested, never deducted**: an approved bill opens on Recover from an advance, filled from the bill's own project's advance first, then its company's — another company's is listed but never suggested — and a cash request shows each contractor's advance against their oldest bills. **Labour with no work order** (kind `nmr`) is offered as miscellaneous labour or a muster roll; a log named instead of a work (Additional work or Rework, `labour_logs.custom_name`) carries its name onto the bill line.
12. **A contractor's bill carries the contractor's number** (`0164`, founder 2026-10-27): `RAJU-PW-ED-08` — the contractor's code (Masters), PW or NMR, the project's company code, and one running count per contractor per company that PW and NMR share. `bills_contractor_reference` mints it on insert for a work-order bill (always PW) or a labour bill with no work order that names a contractor (`bills.work_type`: PW when it is piece-work with no daily wages — `create_nmr_bill`'s `p_piece_work`, `isPieceWorkBill` in `lib/bills/lines.ts`; Send to Bill's direct piece-work passes it). A material bill, or a muster roll paid directly, keeps `BILL/…`. A project with no company, a company with no code or a contractor with no code is refused in a sentence. The count starts after the last paper number typed in Masters (`contractor_bill_numbers`); a deleted bill burns its number, as `BILL/` does. Send to Bill and New bill show the number before it is sent and name any refusal first (`nextContractorBill` in `lib/masters/contractor-bill-number.ts`, mirroring the trigger); the typed muster roll reference is the contractor's own, kept as `invoice_no`, and optional on a contractor's bill (`0165`, founder 2026-10-27: contractors hand over no number) — the box shows the bill's number, and left untouched it is sent blank so the number the database mints fills it. A material bill, a muster roll paid directly or a vendor who is not a contractor still needs one.

## Things that will bite

- **A guard reads as the person saving the row** — `0106`'s read the PO lines and refused every material line to the billing team (BUGCATCHER #19, fixed by `0111`). Test a bill write with `scripts/trials/bills-as-billing-team.sql`.
- **The PO reference on a bill comes from `po_facts`, never an embedded `purchase_orders` join.** The embed silently nulls for `/bills`-only users, because RLS filters the joined row rather than erroring.
- **Transition updates carry no `.eq("status")` filter.** A stale button then gets `bills_guard`'s message instead of a silent zero-row "success". This is the PO lesson; don't reintroduce the filter as an optimisation.
- **`countBillsPipeline` is deliberately ungated** — it reads `bill_facts` only, because the Overview renders for everyone. It is the one exception to "every function opens `requireTool("/bills")`".
- **Bills is a leaf.** Nothing reads it except the fact views. Financial Management reads `bill_money_facts`, never these tables.
