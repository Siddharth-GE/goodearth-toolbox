## Platform

- **Stack:** Next.js 16.2 (Turbopack) · React 19.2 · Tailwind 4 · Supabase Postgres. Vercel `bom1` + Supabase `ap-south-1`, both Mumbai.
- **Migrations:** `0001`–`0093` on both databases; `0094`–`0153` on staging only. `applied_migrations` (`0067`) is the ledger, and CI checks it on every pull request.
- **Access:** per-user grants (`user_apps`) + role bundles (`role_apps`), enforced in the database by `has_app()`. On staging, a tool the founder makes `scoped` (Client Relations) shows each person only their own rows, and admins and its team leads everything (`0122`, `is_lead()`). Every read asks for the emailed code, not just the password, and the public key can call nothing (`0129`).
- **Navigation** (staging): tools sit under closed teams in the sidebar, arranged by the founder in Settings → Teams (`0114`, `lib/nav/`); a tool with no team sits under Other. Teams arrange, grants decide.
- **Sign-in:** password → emailed 6-digit code (30-day trusted device), self-service reset, Google for existing team emails only; both steps rate-limited in the database. Mail rides Resend as `toolbox@goodearthkannur.org`. The rules are `SECURITY.md`.
- **Phone app** (`mobile/PLAN.md`, Android): today's screens, Google and fingerprint sign-in, the camera on picture uploads, and push notifications (staging, `0130`) for an approved indent, a baton reaching its holder, and a design stage handed over.
- **Performance:** warm TTFB ~0.2s, cold ~1.0s — cold starts are the one measured problem.
- **Look:** the Kaadal look (on staging), rules in `DESIGN.md`.
- **Phone app** (staging): `mobile/`, a Capacitor 8 Android app around `toolbox.kaadalgroup.com`, with prints opened in the phone's viewer and Google sign-in through a Chrome tab; version 2.0.0 is built and side-loaded, not yet on Play (`mobile/PLAN.md`). The simpler Play wrapper, version 1, waits for the founder's upload (`android/PLAN.md`).

## Tools

Production unless marked. Each tool's rules are its `PLAN.md` (Google Chat door: `lib/google-chat/PLAN.md`).

- **Masters** — the shared reference data every tool reads: projects, plots, units, clients, vendors, stores, items (catalogue and materials), categories, brands, GST rates, construction stages, units of measure, works, item requests (on staging: companies, terms templates, a material rate that rises with POs, a GST % per material that POs offer first, works grouped in five families, build progress beside construction stages, and an admin's Delete for a master nothing uses — anything in use is retired with Active, `0150`). Reads ungated, writes need `/masters`.
- **Settings** — people, grants, roles, approver lists (on staging: the sidebar's teams, a team lead tick beside a scoped grant, and **Test project** — a made-up villa filled through every tool, with one red button that deletes it). Admin only.
- **Turing** (staging) — the admins' Claude, first in the sidebar: a chat that reads every tool as the admin sees it, takes dropped PDFs, photos and spreadsheets, works out answers over thousands of rows in code, and builds pages any admin can open with fresh data. Each admin's conversations are their own. It proposes changes — Supervisors' schedule and labour logs, and Masters' records, a new one that looks like an existing one flagged — as a table the admin edits and saves through each tool's own saves; nothing is written without the admin's Save (`plan.md`, T2; more tools to follow). Admin only.
- **Selections** — what design specifies per room; revisions immutable once issued. Tiles and lines show the item's picture, description and product link (catalogue pictures on staging).
- **Budgets** — prices an issued interiors revision; money gated. Construction is costed in the Estimator, not here.
- **Indents** — site requests from a villa's official estimate, an approved interiors budget, or direct. No money (on staging: the indent's work's own materials, always in the Masters unit, what is still to buy, and a print; a line no estimate or budget covers says why and waits for the estimator's yes before the indent can be approved).
- **Purchase Orders** — from approved indents, or direct for bulk/urgent buys. The money entry point, gated (on staging: only from indents — a bulk buy is one general PO gathering several villas' indents; made from an indent with a vendor per line, discount and charges per line, CGST + SGST or IGST, terms from a template, the company on the print).
- **Inventory** — goods in, stock issues, adjustments, stock by location, the supervisors' request queue. Quantities, open to every signed-in person (on staging: every store delivery line is a batch carrying its PO rate, issues draw the oldest batch first, and only `/inventory` holders see the rates).
- **Bills** — PO, work-order and NMR bills (on staging: bills have lines and print; labour logs become bills with Send to Bill; work orders replace labour contracts; payments, advances and the weekly cash request).
- **Relay** — the baton relay: trails, departments, schedules, a wave per villa. Stores no documents. The **Google Chat door** drives it from Chat (staging).
- **Design Management** — drawing sets, revisions and transmittals per villa; released drawings reach Supervisors (on staging: a revision's drawings are a Zoho WorkDrive share link, whose folder the toolbox reads — every sheet by name on the cover sheet and on site, the list kept on Issue; TB numbers; the designer sets the revision number on a draft; nothing uploads; and **the Board** — each villa passed from stage to stage, Design to Interiors, with several people on each stage, send-backs for revision that never move a villa back, a history the database writes, and the design team's Actions).
- **Estimator** — what a villa costs: a rate book, each villa measured on its own, a frozen official copy, a site check across villas (the rework on staging; there, Site check opens with the indent lines waiting for the estimator, then a card per villa to open, and a SketchUp model's takeoff measures a villa's estimate — **From the model**).
- **Supervisors** — the site's day on a phone: labour, material requests, drawn-vs-estimated per work (on staging: labour as daily wages or piece-work, a request off the estimate asks why, the site confirms each issue or site delivery line by line with any shortage and why — which Inventory shows on its notes — and site checklists — run one check per screen, Pass, Fail or N/A with a remark and a photo per check, one attachment, signed off as the record; the templates kept under Supervisors → Checklists; and each villa's **programme** — its stages in site order, started by the first labour, marked waiting or done with Undo, its health, what is now and next with the material ready for it, and who runs it, `0137`; the villas as **a board** by stage family with each one's health, a **look ahead** of what starts in the next three weeks with its material and contractor clashes, and **its schedule** — forecast dates worked out from Supervisors → Sequence, the villa's start date and each stage's days, learned once three villas finish a stage, a first plan to measure slip against, and a date strip).
- **Client Relations** — the plot register and the only money coming in: payment schedules and receipts (on staging: the Saarang tracker brought in — **Actions**, every promise to a client with an owner and a date, overdue first; a Plots grid of lot cards; each plot's deed, agreement, build progress, design checks and interiors as one-tap chips; each team member sees only the plots and actions assigned to them, admins and team leads everything; each plot has a Construction side and an Interior side, each with one owner who holds every open step on it).
- **Business Planning** — the founder's JV business plan as a recalculating tool.
- **Financial Management** — cash, forward view and funding facilities, read from views.
- **Reporter** — a report builder over a dataset registry; carries money by founder decision (on staging: a search box, a totals row and quick filters).
- **Directory** — everyone who works here; people keep their own details current.
- **Dexter** — client presentations as shareable links; a deck can carry questions and keeps the client's answers, and every link shows the Kaadal mark when shared on WhatsApp (staging only).
- **Birdwing Stays** (staging) — the guest house's bookings: a room calendar no booking can double, charges, GST invoices numbered by the database (MEF/GH/133 on), dated receipts, and Goodearth's own stays closed to a cost centre. The old income sheet is imported (220 stays) and waits on its Review screen.
- **Birdwing Books** (staging) — the guest house's expenses with their bills, daily attendance, and one confirmed salary per person per month from a dated pay rule. The old expenses workbook and attendance are imported.
- **Training** — stub.

The chain runs end to end: design → price → indent → PO → goods in / stock / goods out → bill → paid.

## The cross-tool read contract

**This table IS the contract.** A column in it can't be renamed or dropped without checking every tool in its row, and a tool that starts reading outside itself adds its row here in the same change. Everything is a `SELECT`; the writes that cross a boundary are the exceptions `SECURITY.md` lists. Masters, `profiles` and `items` are shared surfaces, not another tool's property, and are not listed.

**A cancelled indent** (`indents.status = 'cancelled'`, `0143`) counts nowhere: every reader of `indents` or `indent_lines` that counts or sums leaves it out (POs and the Estimator's queue read only approved or submitted ones); Reporter shows it with its status. A withdrawn estimate and a cancelled work order (`0144`) leave every view by the same road: the views read `status = 'submitted'` and `'approved'` only.

**Nothing reads** Financial Management, Dexter, the Birdwing tools' or the Estimator's own tables — Birdwing only through `birdwing_occupancy_facts` and `0117`'s three facts views; the Estimator only through its views: the rate-free `estimate_takeoff_facts`, `estimate_work_facts` and `work_unit_facts`, and `work_labour_rate_facts` (Bills, gated).
